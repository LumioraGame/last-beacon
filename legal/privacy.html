Last Beacon · 最后的灯塔

Privacy policy / 隐私政策 · Effective November 6, 2026

Your game stays yours

Last Beacon is an offline game. It has no account system, advertising, purchases, analytics SDK, or automatic developer-operated crash-reporting service. The game does not send your gameplay, saved progress, settings, identifiers, or diagnostic logs to the developer.

Local storage and backups

The app saves the current watch, best records, language, graphics quality, and tutorial choices in app storage. A small local diagnostic log may contain the app version, platform, graphics mode, and technical errors. Your operating system or browser may include this storage in backups according to your own settings. Data can be lost when app storage is cleared or the app is removed, and an operating-system backup may restore it.

Support you choose to send

Sharing diagnostics is optional and starts only when you choose the share action. Review the text before sharing it. If you contact us through the project's issue tracker, the information you submit is handled by GitHub and may be public. Please do not include personal or sensitive information. Opening external support pages uses the hosting provider's normal web services.

Control and contact

Starting a new watch replaces the current saved run. To remove all app data, use Android's Clear storage option, remove the app on iOS, or clear the site's storage in your browser; review your backup settings if you also want to remove backups. Support and privacy questions: Last Beacon issue tracker. Store platforms may process installation, purchase-account, and platform crash information under their own policies.

你的守望，由你掌握

《最后的灯塔》是一款离线游戏，无账号系统、广告、内购、统计 SDK 或由开发者运营的自动崩溃上报服务。游戏不会向开发者发送玩法记录、存档、设置、标识符或诊断日志。

本地存储与备份

应用在本地保存当前守望、最高纪录、语言、画质与引导选择。少量本地诊断信息可能包括版本、平台、画质和技术错误。操作系统或浏览器可能根据你的设置备份这些数据。清除应用数据或移除应用会导致数据丢失，系统备份也可能恢复这些数据。

由你选择提供的支持信息

诊断信息仅在你主动选择分享时提供，请先检查分享内容。通过项目问题追踪器提交的信息由 GitHub 处理，且可能公开，请勿提交个人或敏感信息。打开外部支持页面会使用该托管服务的常规网络服务。

管理与联系

开始新的一局会替换当前存档。删除全部本地数据可使用 Android 的清除存储选项、在 iOS 移除应用，或在浏览器清除网站数据。如需同时删除备份，请检查系统备份设置。支持和隐私问题请前往游戏问题追踪器。应用商店可能依据自身政策处理安装、平台账号和崩溃信息。

Last Beacon support · 最后的灯塔支持

Continue a watch

When you return, choose Continue watch. The game restores the latest successful checkpoint and stays paused until you resume. Backgrounding pauses combat. A sudden process termination may lose a few recent seconds; a visible save warning means your latest progress is not yet safely stored.

Graphics and sound

Open the menu to lower graphics quality or choose 2D on older devices. If graphics are interrupted, wait for recovery; the game can switch to 2D while keeping your watch paused. Sound starts off. Enable it with the music button. On iOS, check the silent switch and system volume. After a call or voice assistant, tap a game control to allow audio to resume.

Report a problem

Use Share diagnostics in the handbook or the recovery screen. Review what you share. Include the app version, device and OS, what you were doing, and whether the problem happens repeatedly. Do not include sensitive information or your full saved game.

继续守望

返回时选择「继续守望」，游戏会读取上次成功保存的记录，并保持暂停直到你继续。切到后台会暂停战斗。进程突然终止时可能丢失最近几秒进度；如果看到保存警告，说明最新进度尚未安全写入。

画质与声音

可在菜单降低画质，旧设备可选择 2D。画面中断时请等待恢复；游戏可能切换为 2D 并保留暂停中的守望。声音默认关闭，点击音符开启。iOS 请检查静音开关与系统音量；通话或语音助手结束后，点击游戏控件可允许声音恢复。

反馈问题

在玩法说明或恢复界面选择「分享诊断信息」，并检查分享内容。请说明应用版本、设备与系统、当时的操作，以及问题能否重复出现。请勿提交敏感信息或完整存档。
